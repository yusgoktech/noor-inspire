Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Summary

Noor Inspire (the “App”) is developed by yusgoktech. The App has no accounts, sign-up, ads, analytics or tracking. All data about your use of the App stays on your device and is never sent to us or to any advertising or analytics company.

Data stored on your device

The following information is kept only on your device, in the App’s own storage:

  • Preferences: theme, language, notification times
  • Daily entries: mood, intention, gratitude selections and your notes
  • 40-day program progress, streaks, level and badges
  • Saved verses, prayers, bookmarks and reading position
  • The location (country/city/district) you select for prayer times and the downloaded times

Information sent over the internet

To show prayer times, the code of the district you select is sent to ezanvakti.emushaf.net, which serves data from the Turkish Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet). The request contains no name, email or device location; as with any internet request, that server can see your IP address.

Apart from this, the App sends no data over the internet.

Location

Location permission is requested “while using the app” only for the Qibla Compass. Your location is used on your device to calculate the Qibla direction and the distance to the Kaaba; it is not stored and never sent to any server. Granting it is optional; prayer times work without it using the district you pick from the list.

Notifications

Morning/evening reminders and prayer time alerts are local notifications scheduled on your device. No notification server is used; you can turn them off at any time in your device settings.

Sharing

When you share a verse, hadith or prayer, the content is passed to the app you choose through your device’s share sheet.

Third parties

The App contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or payment SDKs.

  • ezanvakti.emushaf.net: prayer times (selected district code only)
  • Apple App Store: app distribution (subject to Apple’s own privacy policy)

Deleting your data

Use Settings > “Delete all my data” to erase all App data on your device. Uninstalling the App also deletes all of its data. Since we do not hold your data, no separate request is needed.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the App’s use of data changes (for example, a new feature), this policy will be updated and the current version published in the App and on the web page.
